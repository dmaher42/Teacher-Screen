Teacher Screen help

  • On the Dashboard, open Deck Library and select a deck to reveal Continue Classroom, Arrange, Present and deck actions.
  • Open Resources from the Dashboard to connect a computer folder or Google Drive and add supported files to the current deck.
  • Open More → Sections to move between Dashboard, Classroom, Planner, Timetable and Notes.
  • Open More → Teacher Controls, or choose Arrange on a deck, to manage the current deck, pages, timer, theme and background.
  • In Classroom, use the bottom quick actions to add Text, Timer, Track, Draw, Pick and other widgets.
  • Decks, pages and classroom changes save automatically in this browser. Resource files stay in their original computer folder or Google Drive unless you add supported material to a deck.
