How to Use Teacher Controls

  • Use the navigation bar to switch between views.
  • Open Resources from the Dashboard to connect a teaching folder or Google Drive and add supported files to the current deck.
  • Use the lesson quick actions to add Text, Timer, Draw, or Pick.
  • Drag any widget by its grab bar; use its options menu for settings or removal.
  • Open Teacher Controls from Menu Desk when you need deck or classroom settings.
  • All changes save automatically to your browser.
